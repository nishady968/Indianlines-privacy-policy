Privacy Policy for Indian Lines

Last updated: 22 November 2026

1. Information We Collect

a) Location: We collect your approximate location to show queue and crowd status near you. You can turn off location permission from settings.

b) Information You Provide: If you report crowd / queue status, create a place, or contact us, we may collect name, crowd level, and your message.

c) Device & Usage: We may collect device model, app version, and crash logs to fix bugs.

d) Account: If you sign up, we may collect email address or phone number to create your account.

2. How We Use Information

To show nearby places, live queue status, improve app, and respond to feedback.

3. What We Do NOT Do

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share your exact live location publicly.

4. Third-Party Services

Our app uses:

Supabase - for database and storage
Firebase / Google - for push notifications and analytics
Google Play Services - for maps and location
Shorebird - for app updates

5. Permissions We Use

Location, Internet, Notifications.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable security measures to protect your data. No method is 100% secure.

7. Disclaimer

Crowd and queue information is user-reported and is for general guidance only. It may not always be 100% accurate.

8. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent. If you find such content, please report it to us.

9. Your Choices

You can turn off location and notification permissions from device settings. You can request deletion of your account/data by contacting us.
